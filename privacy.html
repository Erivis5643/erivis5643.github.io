Privacy.

Last updated November 2026.

Information stored

If you use an account, the service uses Supabase Authentication for your email/password sign-in and stores your username, optional profile bio, and workouts you create. With email confirmation disabled for this small group setup, the app does not verify that an account owner controls the email address; choose a unique password and avoid relying on email-based password recovery until SMTP is configured. Workouts you choose to make public can be viewed by anyone. Private workouts and your history are restricted to your account by database access policies.

Local mode

Without a connected Supabase project, workout history is stored in this browser's local storage. It is not synced and may be removed when browser data is cleared.

Service providers

The app uses Supabase for authentication and database hosting when configured, and Google Fonts for typography. This version does not load advertising scripts or show ads.

Your choices

You can keep workouts private, save them to your own library, or explicitly publish them. Avoid including sensitive personal information in public workout descriptions. For account or data deletion requests, use the contact details on the Contact page.
